# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Give feedback on the work, not the person. No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks. Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders.

## Reporting
Report a problem privately to the maintainers at Elix Innovations (Pvt) Ltd through [elix.lk](https://elix.lk), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request. Reports are kept confidential.
